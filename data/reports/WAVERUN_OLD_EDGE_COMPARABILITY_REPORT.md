# Old edge comparability

V1 ~60% later OOS, V5.3 87.2%, and V6 ~14% are not comparable. They differ in target magnitude, horizon, direction conditioning, selection, de-clustering, and OOS status. V5.3 is $100 MFE in 60m on a selected SHORT discovery population; V6 is directional $100 within 300s on a broad fast-move population; V1 used a different direction/endpoint and proxy-cost contract. The apparent gap is therefore a mixture of selection, horizon, and base rate, not direct evidence of a strategy edge.

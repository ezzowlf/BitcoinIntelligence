# WAVERUN winner forensics master report

The frozen V5.3 population was reconstructed without changing selection: 47 SHORT candidates, 41 winners, 26 reaching $500 MFE, 6 reaching $300, 9 reaching $100, and 6 absorbed/failure cases. Winner median MFE was $835.61, MAE -$533.87, and time to $100 was 55s. Loser median MFE was $76.90 and MAE -$899.32.

Winner location descriptors: 1h range-position median 0.100 and daily-VWAP distance -30.56bp. Losers: 0.032 and -0.14bp. This suggests lower-range context plus displacement from VWAP may matter, but N=6 losers is insufficient for effect-size claims.

The evidence supports studying Direction + Location + Timing as a future research architecture, but not freezing V7.
